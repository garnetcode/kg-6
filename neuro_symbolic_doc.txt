A neuro-symbolic agent combines neural networks with symbolic reasoning. It integrates Large Language Models with Knowledge Graphs. Knowledge Graphs store explicit factual knowledge as structured entities and relationships. LLMs provide natural language understanding capabilities. The neuro-symbolic alliance creates enterprise AI systems that have both language fluency and factual grounding. This architecture enables capabilities that neither technology can achieve in isolation. Knowledge Graphs provide structure, verifiability, and context. LLMs enable natural language interaction and content generation.
